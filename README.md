# Privacy Policy for PDF Compressor

**Effective Date:** August 18, 2026

## 1. Overview
PDF Compressor ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our desktop application processes information when you use PDF Compressor on Windows.

## 2. Information We Do Not Collect
- **No Personal Data Collection:** We do not collect, capture, store, or request any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, IP address, or location.
- **No File Uploads or Cloud Transmissions:** All PDF files, documents, and images selected or processed through PDF Compressor remain entirely on your local Windows device. Your files are never uploaded, transmitted, or stored on any remote servers or cloud services.
- **No Analytics or Tracking:** The application does not contain telemetry, tracking scripts, or third-party analytics services.

## 3. Local Storage and Data Usage
PDF Compressor uses local storage strictly on your device to store:
- Daily compression quota usage counters.
- Pro license unlock status.

This data is stored solely on your local device and is never transmitted off your machine.

## 4. Third-Party Services
PDF Compressor does not share any data with third-party advertising, marketing, or data broker networks.

## 5. Security
Because all PDF compression tasks take place locally on your personal device, your privacy and data security are governed by your device's own security settings and local file system permissions.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected with a revised effective date.
